What Are Dentures?

Dentures are a removable alternative to replace missing teeth and surrounding tissues. They help support the oral cavity, improve facial muscles, and restore balance to your bite. Dentures are custom designed to fit each wearer’s mouth, offering both function and a natural appearance.

There are different types of dentures, depending on your dental needs. You may need partial dentures if you still have some healthy teeth or complete dentures if you’re replacing all teeth in the upper or lower jaw.

Types of Dentures We Offer

At our Baton Rouge clinic, we offer several denture solutions designed to meet a range of needs:

Complete Dentures

These are used when all natural teeth are missing in the upper or lower jaw. Full dentures rest directly on the gums and help support facial muscles, improving both look and function.

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are used when some natural teeth remain. They can be supported by a metal framework that clasps to the remaining teeth. Partial dentures rest comfortably in the wearer’s mouth and help prevent other teeth from shifting.

Immediate Dentures

These are temporary dentures placed the same day your natural teeth are removed. They help maintain your appearance during healing while your custom-made set is created.

Implant Supported Dentures

These combine the benefits of dental implants with dentures. Anchored by implants placed in the jawbone, implant supported dentures offer more stability, especially for the lower arch. Many patients prefer this option because it reduces movement and improves bite strength.

Premium Dentures

We also provide premium dentures made with stain resistant, natural color materials that closely match the look of natural teeth. These are built for comfort, durability, and a confident smile.

Benefits of Getting Dentures

Choosing the right dentures comes with several long-term benefits:

  • Restore full function for eating and speaking
  • Improve support for facial muscles
  • Enhance your natural appearance
  • Support for remaining teeth in the case of partial dentures
  • Custom fit for comfort and stability
  • Options to match your lifestyle and oral health needs
  • Reduce jawbone loss when combined with dental implants

We use high-quality materials to create a set that looks like natural teeth and fits your mouth perfectly.

Our Denture Process: From First Visit to Final Fit

At Elements Dental Spa & Aesthetics, we follow a personalized approach for every denture patient.

  1. Consultation – We examine your oral cavity and discuss your needs, goals, and lifestyle.
  2. Impressions – We take accurate molds of your mouth to design a set that fits just right.
  3. Try In – You’ll test a wax version of your dentures to check fit, bite, and natural appearance.
  4. Custom Creation – Your dentures are crafted in a trusted dental laboratory using high quality materials.
  5. Fitting – We make final adjustments to ensure a comfortable fit and balanced bite.
  6. Follow-up – We schedule post-fitting visits to make sure your dentures remain secure and functional.

Denture Repairs, Relines, and Replacements

Dentures wear over time. If your current set feels loose, damaged, or uncomfortable, we offer:

  • Denture repairs for chips, cracks, or breaks
  • Relines to adjust fit and reduce pressure spots
  • Full replacements if your existing set no longer functions well
  • Adjustment services for partial dentures rest and balance

We work quickly to get you back to smiling with minimal disruption.

Average Cost of Dentures in Louisiana

The cost of dentures in Louisiana depends on the type, materials, and any extra dental work. On average:

  • Full Dentures: $1,000–$3,500 per arch
  • Partial Dentures: $700–$2,000
  • Immediate Dentures: $1,500–$3,000
  • Implant Supported Dentures: $3,000–$6,000 (per arch, excluding implant surgery)
  • Premium Dentures: $2,500–$5,000+

Prices vary based on the use of implant supported systems, tooth extractions, and customization level. High-end materials like stain-resistant finishes and natural color design can also affect the cost.

We offer flexible options and can discuss payment plans during your consultation.

Denture Care and Maintenance

Taking care of your dentures is just as important as getting the right fit. With proper care, your dentures can last longer, feel better, and help protect your oral cavity, facial muscles, and remaining teeth.

We recommend cleaning your dentures every day using a soft denture brush and a non-abrasive cleanser. Rinse them after meals and soak them overnight in a denture cleaning solution, unless advised otherwise. Avoid using regular toothpaste, which can scratch the surface.

Always handle your dentures carefully. Clean them over a towel or soft surface in case they slip. Never try to adjust them yourself—bring them in for professional repairs if needed.

Don’t forget your mouth. Brush your gums, tongue, and any natural teeth gently to keep your oral cavity healthy. Rinse your mouth before putting dentures back in.

Regular follow-up visits are important. We check the fit, make adjustments, and recommend relining or replacing your dentures when needed—usually every 5 to 10 years. With the right care, your dentures will continue to support a confident smile and a natural appearance.

FAQs

How long do dentures last?

Most dentures last 5 to 10 years with proper care. Over time, your mouth shape may change, and a reline or replacement may be needed.

Will dentures affect how I eat or speak?

There is an adjustment period, but most patients adapt quickly. We provide guidance on how to practice speaking and eating.

Can I sleep with my dentures in?

Dentures should be removed overnight to allow your gums to rest and reduce bacteria buildup.

Do dentures look like real teeth?

Yes. Our premium dentures are made to match the natural color and shape of natural teeth, giving you a real and confident look.

Can I get dentures if I still have some healthy teeth?

Yes. Partial dentures are designed to work with your remaining teeth and keep your bite balanced.
