Kids Cleanings

A child’s early experiences at the dentist can shape their feelings about oral care for life.

Book a consultation (225) 398-8812

Some children feel nervous about dental visits, so we focus on building trust and making every appointment a positive experience. Our goal is to help kids develop healthy habits early on, setting them up for a lifetime of good oral health.

If you’re looking for a kid-friendly dental office that provides gentle, high-quality care, contact us today to schedule an appointment.

When to Start Dental Cleanings

Routine cleanings help prevent cavities and keep teeth healthy. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) recommends that children visit the dentist six months after their first tooth appears or by their first birthday. Regular dental cleanings should begin by age three, with checkups scheduled every six months to keep their teeth in good condition.

Children with higher risks of cavities or other dental concerns may need more frequent visits. Regular checkups allow us to catch potential issues before they become bigger problems.

What to Expect at a Cleaning

At Elements Dental Spa & Aesthetics, we make every dental cleaning in Baton Rouge gentle, thorough, and stress-free. Our team explains each step to help kids feel comfortable and involved.

Here’s what happens during a kids’ dental cleaning:

  • Plaque and Tartar Removal: A gentle cleaning removes plaque and tartar buildup, helping to prevent cavities and gum problems.
  • Polishing Teeth: Teeth are polished to remove surface stains and create a smooth finish.
  • Fluoride Treatment: Fluoride is applied to strengthen enamel and protect against decay.
  • Oral Health Check: The dentist examines your child’s teeth and gums for early signs of cavities or alignment issues.
  • Dental Care Tips: We guide children on proper brushing and flossing and discuss habits that support oral health.

We use kid-friendly language and a calm approach to make each visit a positive experience.

Why Regular Cleanings Matter for Kids

Routine cleanings do more than just keep teeth clean. They help prevent cavities, gum disease, and other problems that could lead to discomfort or costly treatments later on.

Here’s why cleanings are beneficial:

  • Prevents Tooth Decay: Plaque buildup can lead to cavities if not removed.
  • Protects Gum Health: Healthy gums support strong teeth and help prevent gum disease.
  • Monitors Growth and Development: Catching alignment issues early allows for easier treatment.
  • Encourages Good Habits: Regular visits help reinforce the importance of brushing and flossing.
  • Reduces Dental Anxiety: Positive experiences help kids feel more comfortable with dental care as they grow.

Starting good dental habits early can save time, money, and discomfort in the long run.

Cavity Prevention

Keeping cavities away involves a combination of good habits at home and professional dental care. Here are some simple ways to help protect your child’s teeth:

  • Brushing: Teach your child to brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste to remove plaque and strengthen enamel.
  • Flossing: Flossing daily cleans between teeth where a toothbrush can’t reach, preventing plaque buildup and cavities.
  • Healthy Eating: Limiting sugary snacks and drinks helps lower the risk of tooth decay.
  • Regular Dental Visits: Schedule cleanings and checkups every six months to keep teeth in good condition.
  • Dental Sealants: Sealants add an extra layer of protection to molars, helping to prevent cavities.

Following these steps will help keep your child’s teeth healthy and cavity-free for years to come.

Dental Sealants

Even with daily brushing, some areas of the teeth are harder to clean, especially the deep grooves in molars. Dental sealants provide extra protection by sealing these grooves, keeping out food and bacteria.

Sealants are a quick, gentle treatment that helps prevent cavities for up to ten years. Our dentist may recommend sealants for children as soon as their permanent molars come in, usually around ages six and twelve.

Fluoride Treatments for Stronger Teeth

Fluoride is a proven method to prevent cavities by strengthening tooth enamel and reversing early signs of decay. We offer fluoride treatments during regular cleanings to give your child’s teeth extra protection.

Fluoride treatments are safe, fast, and effective, making them an easy way to support long-term oral health.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should children start getting dental cleanings?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) recommends that children visit the dentist six months after their first tooth appears or by their first birthday. Regular dental cleanings should begin by age three, with checkups scheduled every six months.

What happens during a kids’ dental cleaning appointment?

During a kids’ dental cleaning, the dentist or hygienist will gently remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish the teeth, apply a fluoride treatment, check for any signs of cavities or alignment issues, and guide proper brushing and flossing techniques.

Why are regular dental cleanings important for children?

Regular dental cleanings help prevent tooth decay, protect gum health, monitor growth and development, encourage good oral hygiene habits, and reduce dental anxiety by providing positive experiences. Starting good dental habits early can save time, money, and discomfort in the long run.

How can I help prevent cavities in my child’s teeth?

To help prevent cavities, teach your child to brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, limit sugary snacks and drinks, schedule regular dental cleanings every six months, and consider dental sealants for added protection on molars.

What are dental sealants and how do they help prevent cavities?

Dental sealants are a thin protective coating applied to the grooves of molars, sealing out food and bacteria that can cause cavities. Sealants are a quick, gentle treatment that can prevent cavities for up to ten years. They are usually recommended for children as soon as their permanent molars come in, around ages six and twelve.

Are fluoride treatments safe for children?

Yes, professional fluoride treatments are widely considered safe and effective for strengthening tooth enamel and preventing cavities in children. The dentist will apply fluoride during regular cleanings to provide extra protection and support long-term oral health.
