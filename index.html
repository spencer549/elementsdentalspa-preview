Your first visit

What actually happens.

Six steps, in order, so nothing on the day is a surprise.

01

Schedule

Call, or use live chat on the site, to schedule an appointment.

02

Confirm and intake

Confirm by text or email and complete digital intake forms. Paper forms are available on request.

03

Check in

Check in at the front desk on the day and bring photo ID with you.

04

Tour and 3D scan

A team member tours you through the office, then takes a 3D scan in the imaging room.

05

Plan together

We plan your recommended care together and discuss finances as needed.

06

Next visit

Your next visit is scheduled and confirmed by text or email.

Your dentist

Dr. Cecilia Luong, DDS

Cosmetic and restorative dentistry

A New Orleans native, Dr. Luong moved to Baton Rouge in the summer of 2013. She earned her BS in Biology from Xavier University and her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the LSU School of Dentistry.

She speaks English and Vietnamese, has been active in her Vietnamese church community all her life, and volunteers at health fairs and patient education events. She routinely attends continuing education courses to stay current with new research and technique.
