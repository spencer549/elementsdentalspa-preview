Implant Dentistry

Missing teeth don’t just change how you look. They can affect how you eat, speak, and even how your other teeth stay in place. Over time, gaps in your smile can lead to shifting teeth and bone loss in your jaw.

Dental Implants

A dental implant is a small, screw-like post made of titanium. It’s placed in your jawbone to act as a replacement for the root of a missing tooth. As your body heals, the implant naturally bonds with the bone, holding it in place.

Once the area is ready, we attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture to complete your smile. The result looks, feels, and functions just like your natural teeth.

Dental implants can restore a single tooth, several teeth, or even a full arch—and they’re built to last.

Why Implants Work Well

Dental implants in Baton Rouge offer more than just a way to fill gaps—they provide long-term comfort, function, and support for your overall oral health.

  • Eat What You Like: Dental implants let you enjoy your favorite meals without worrying about shifting or discomfort.
  • Smile with Ease: The restoration that goes on top is color-matched and shaped to blend with your natural teeth.
  • Protect Your Other Teeth: Implants don’t rely on surrounding teeth for support, so those teeth stay strong and untouched.
  • Help Prevent Bone Loss: Tooth loss often leads to jawbone shrinkage. Dental implants replace the root and help keep your jaw healthy.
  • Built to Stay: With proper care, dental implants can last for many years, often decades.
  • High Success Rate: Today’s dental implants have a long track record of success, with most performing well over time.

Who Implants Suit

Most adults with missing teeth may be able to get dental implants. During your consultation, we’ll review your health and goals before making a recommendation.

You may be ready for dental implants if:

  • Your gums are healthy
  • Your jawbone is strong enough to hold the implant
  • You’re in stable overall health

Other options may be discussed if:

  • You’re still growing (teens or children)
  • You have uncontrolled health conditions like diabetes
  • You smoke frequently, which may slow healing

We’ll walk through everything during your first visit and help you choose the most effective path forward.

The Implant Process

At Element Dental Spa, here’s what you can expect:

  1. Consultation and Planning: We start with a full exam, including digital scans and X-rays. From there, we create a plan tailored to your needs.
  2. Placement of the Implant: A titanium post is placed in your jawbone under local anesthesia. The procedure is typically easy to recover from—many return to work the following day.
  3. Healing and Bonding: The implant bonds with your jawbone over 4 to 6 months. This step is what gives the implant its long-term strength.
  4. Final Restoration: After the implant is secure, we attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture. It’s made to blend in with your natural teeth for a smooth, complete smile.

How Long Implants Last

The titanium implant can last for many years, often decades, with proper care. It becomes part of your jaw through a natural healing process and usually does not need to be replaced.

The crown or bridge that sits on top may wear out after 10–20 years. If that happens, we can replace the top part without affecting the implant itself.

Regular checkups and good brushing and flossing habits will help keep your implant in top condition.

Cost of Dental Implants

The cost of your dental implant treatment depends on several things, such as:

  • How many teeth are being replaced
  • The type of restoration used
  • Whether any prep work is needed (like bone grafting)

At your first appointment, we’ll go over everything and give you a clear breakdown of the pricing.

Implant-Supported Dentures for Full-Arch Tooth Replacement

If you’re missing most or all of your teeth, implant-supported dentures can provide stability and long-term comfort. These dentures are anchored by multiple implants placed along the jaw for a secure fit.

Compared to traditional dentures, implant-supported ones don’t move when you eat or speak. They also help your jawbone stay strong and preserve your facial shape.

We work closely with a trusted oral surgeon in Baton Rouge for precise placement, and we handle the rest at Element Dental Spa.

Alternatives to Implants

We also offer alternative solutions:

Dental Bridges

These replace one or more teeth by using nearby healthy teeth for support. They’re stable and long-lasting but don’t offer the same bone support as implants.

Full and Partial Dentures

Dentures rest on the gums and can be removed for cleaning. While they are often more affordable, they may require more frequent adjustments or replacements over time.
